# Status

COMPLETE_DISPROVED

Completed 2026-11-04. Independent Weibull variables with survival `S(x) = exp(-x^(3/2))` and mean `mu = Gamma(5/3)` satisfy `P(X+Y >= 2z, min(X,Y) <= z) / P(min(X,Y) >= z) < 2z/mu` for every `z > 0`. This disproves the mean-normalized existence assertion in Section 1.3 of Steinerberger's *A sharp estimate for probability distributions*.

review, complete rendered-page inspection, and a fresh-extraction run.
